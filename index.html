Complexity




m = 4n, toh m² = 16n². Har cell ek baar hi visit hoti hai, toh time = O(m²) = O(n²).


Isse faster nahi ja sakte, kyunki output mein hi saari 16n² cells return karni hain.


Auxiliary space = O(n²) (visited). Output space = O(n²) (dono coils). Ye O(1) space solution nahi hai.
